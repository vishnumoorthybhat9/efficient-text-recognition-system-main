Unit - II
Capital budgeting is connected to the long term inves-
-tment decision with primary focus on the objective of
maximizing bonus wealth. The features of capital
budgeting include large anticipated benifits on inves-
-tment, high degree of risk, long time period b/w. initial
outlay (expenditure) & anticipated returns / benifits.
The benifits are expected to occur over series of yrs.
Types of investment decisions can be categorized under
capital budgeting are :
a. Expansion & diverification
b. Replacement & Modernization
c. Mutually exclusive investments
d. Independent investments
e. Contingent investments
Traditional / Non - discounted methods
1. Payback period methods
Payback period is the exact time required to
recover the initial investment in a project.
Payback period = Initial Investment / Annual cash inflows = I / A
2. Discounted Payback period
No. of years in recovering a cash outlay or initial
investment on the present value basis.
= Initial Investment / Discounted present payback value of annual cash flows.
